# TrustScore
Universal on-chain reputation score derived from staking accuracy across the knowledge graph.
## Problem

TrustScore aims to solve the lack of a universal on-chain reputation score for the decentralized web, providing users with a reliable 'credit score' system to assess trustworthiness in the decentralized space.

## Proposed Solution

TrustScore offers a decentralized reputation score derived from staking accuracy across the knowledge graph, enabling users to evaluate the trustworthiness of entities on the decentralized web.

## Target Users

Users in decentralized ecosystems, such as DeFi participants, NFT traders, and DAO members, who require reliable reputation metrics to make informed decisions.

## Intuition Integration

Intuition is crucial for TrustScore as it involves semantic modeling of reputation data, staking mechanisms, and knowledge graph interactions to derive accurate and trustworthy reputation scores for decentralized users.

## MVP

The MVP for TrustScore would include a basic staking mechanism, reputation score calculation based on accuracy, and a simple user interface to view and interpret reputation scores.

## Challenge Notes

Ensuring widespread adoption and understanding of the staking mechanisms and reputation scoring algorithms is crucial for the success of TrustScore.
