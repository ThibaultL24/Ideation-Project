Intuition Primitives (Quick Reference)
Primitive

What It Does

Atoms

Identities for any entity — person, org, contract, product, concept

Triples

Claims connecting atoms: [Subject] [Predicate] [Object]

Vaults

Smart contracts holding staked $TRUST per atom/triple

Bonding Curves

Automatic price discovery on the "truth value" of any claim

Counter-Staking

Staking against a claim — disagreement has economic cost

Knowledge Graph The universal, permissionless graph of all atoms and triples

Why Intuition changes everything: Every review, rating, credential, and claim
gets financial skin-in-the-game. Fake reviews cost real money. Honest curation
earns real money. Reputation becomes portable. Trust becomes a market.
