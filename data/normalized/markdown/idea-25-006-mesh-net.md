Intuition's bonding curves and counter-staking make astroturfing prohibitively expensive and honest early curation profitable. 3. Portable Reputation — Your reputation on Uber doesn't transfer to Lyft. Your Airbnb hosting reviews don't help on VRBO. Counter-Staking as Accountability — The ability to financially stake AGAINST a claim is as important as staking for it.
